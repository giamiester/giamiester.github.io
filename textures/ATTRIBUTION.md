# Planet texture attribution

The bundled planetary image maps were downloaded from the Solar System Scope texture pack:

- Mercury: `2k_mercury.jpg`
- Venus atmosphere: `2k_venus_atmosphere.jpg`
- Earth day map: `2k_earth_daymap.jpg`
- Mars: `2k_mars.jpg`
- Jupiter: `2k_jupiter.jpg`
- Saturn: `2k_saturn.jpg`
- Uranus: `2k_uranus.jpg`
- Neptune: `2k_neptune.jpg`

Source: <https://phet.solarsystemscope.com/textures/>  
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.  
The maps are based on NASA elevation and imagery data. Some unmapped areas are filled, and colors are enhanced, as described on the source page.
